How to Use

  1. Use twice a week.
  2. Take 5–6 drops in your palm, section your hair, and apply to the scalp.
  3. Massage with firm pressure for 10 minutes. Circular motions, not a light pat — the scalp needs the pressure, not just the oil.
  4. Leave it in for 10–15 minutes before washing. Longer isn't required; it's not marinating.

Dry hair

Lean toward more drops and the longer end of the wait time — your hair can take the extra oil and needs it.

Oily hair

Stick closer to 5 drops and the shorter wait — adjust down rather than skipping oiling altogether.

The Fine Print (read it, we mean it)

This oil is genuinely nourishing — used consistently, it strengthens and visibly improves what you already have. What it won't do is edit your genetics. Inherited hair loss patterns don't reverse because of a bottle, however well-blended.

It's also not a substitute for washing your hair. Oil over days of scalp buildup isn't treatment, it's a marinade. Cleanse, then oil — order matters as much as the massage.

And it won't manage your stress, sleep, or diet for you. Hair reflects the body it grows out of — no oil out-argues a skipped meal or a string of 3am nights. Use it as the best-supporting actor your hair will ever have, not the whole cast.
